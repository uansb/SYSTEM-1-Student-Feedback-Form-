Welcome to the ITECH Student Feedback Portal, a dedicated space for every student to share their insights and experiences regarding our learning environment. At the Institute of Technology, we believe that your voice is the most vital tool for institutional growth, and we are committed to ensuring that every concern-form is state of our facilities and classroom cleanliness to academic experiences-is heard and addressed. By participating in this survey, you are taking an active role in shaping a better, more efficient, and more supportive department for yourself and your fellow students. Your honest feedback is the foundation upon which we will build a stronger community, turning individual observations into collective progress for our second home.

ABOUT US


We are a dedicated group of five Information Technology students from PUP Institute of Technology (ITECH), currently collaborating on this project as part of our Web Development course under the guidance of our professor, Jonnalyn Apsay. Our team is composed of Jhon Carl Gabarda, Ryan Libres, Spike Palagam, John Marvin Soleybar, Euan Alfonso Suba, and John Felix Sungahid. Driven by the principles of user-centered design, our primary mission is to create and maintain a digital environment where the voices of our fellow students are not only heard but serve as a catalyst for meaningful change. We recognize that a better campus starts with open communication, and we are committed to providing a transparent, accessible platform that bridges the gap between student concerns and departmental improvement.
